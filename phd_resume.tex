\headingsimple{Typed Process Reward Models for Medical Image Reasoning}{\textit{Jun 2026 -- Present}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Supervising medical image reasoning step by step, checking whether each step is grounded in the image, whether its visual claims are correct, and whether its clinical inferences hold
  \item Building an automated pipeline that labels reasoning chains sampled from Qwen2.5-VL-7B; the trained model will be used for Best-of-N selection and as step-level rewards in GRPO to train models that look at the image and reason correctly
\end{itemize}

\headingsimple{Audio‑based Emotion Detection for Low‑Resource Language}{\textit{Aug 2025 -- Dec 2025}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Studied how large pre-trained speech models can detect emotion in Bangla, a low-resource language where labeled emotional speech is scarce
  \item Fine-tuned Whisper and HuBERT on a small labeled Bangla emotion dataset, then applied semi-supervised adaptation on a large unlabeled Bangla audio corpus, improving emotion-detection accuracy over supervised fine-tuning alone
\end{itemize}


\headingsimple{LLM-Grounded Feedback for Dance Motion Analysis}{\textit{Jan 2023 -- Dec 2025}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Built an automated feedback system for modern dance that compares student performances against instructor references using 3D skeletal sequences, identifies movement deviations, and generates personalized corrective guidance using an LLM
  \item Extracted 3D poses via YOLO-Pose and stereo depth, classified movements with a fine-tuned pretrained skeletal transformer, aligned student-instructor sequences using FastDTW to compute joint-level deviations, and used Gemini with the measured deviations to generate actionable corrective feedback
\end{itemize}
